# [E0103]
---
## Title: Impossible triangles
### Classification: 🟠 Euclid
- Impossible triangles are basically triangles whose sides have a value less than or equal to 0 (s <= 0). This error can also be encountered for angles less than or equal to 0° (∠A <= 0°).

## Possible Encounters
- `sin_law()`
- `cos_law()`

## Tips
- Make sure the parameters representing the sides and angles of a triangle are not mathematically close and less than 0.
