## Title: Negative Discriminant Error
### Classification: 🟠 Euclid
- This error appears if b² - 4ac < 0. And according to the laws of mathematics, a quadratic formula with a negative discriminant has roots that are unequal and imaginary which means there are no real-number solutions to the equation. Instead, its roots are a pair of complex conjugates, consisting of a real component and an imaginary component.

## Possible Encounters:
- `quadratic_formula()`

## Tips:
- Change the equation in a way where D >= 0
